Carbon markets pay. Traders are locked out.

The Solution

How SokoCarbon works

01
Enrol via USSD
Dial *384# on any phone. No smartphone, no data, no bank account needed.
02
Log weekly sales
Trader enters clean fuel type and quantity each week via 2-button menu.
03
Emissions calculated
Gold Standard TPDDTEC engine calculates each trader's avoided CO₂ automatically.
04
Credits pool
Thousands of traders' micro-credits aggregate until the 100-tonne bundle threshold is met.
05
Bundle listed
Verified bundle appears on buyer dashboard. Corporates and NGOs purchase directly.
06
Paid via mobile money
Revenue disbursed proportionally to each trader via Airtel Money or TNM Mpamba. Instant.

USSD Interface

Works on any phone.
Any network. No data.

SokoCarbon is built for the actual infrastructure traders have — a 2G feature phone and a mobile money account. No smartphone. No app download. No bank account required.

  • USSD *384# — works on Airtel, TNM, Safaricom, MTN
  • 2-button navigation, Chichewa/Swahili/English support
  • Emissions calculated automatically in background
  • Payouts to Airtel Money & TNM Mpamba
  • PDF carbon certificate emailed or printed on request
